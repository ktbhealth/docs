KTB Health is a documentation tool used by clinicians — not a patient-facing
app. If your doctor uses it, here's what that looks like from your side.

## During the visit

- Your clinician may ask for your **consent** before the visit is recorded or
  transcribed. This is required — nothing is captured without it.
- With consent, a transcription tool may listen to the conversation to help
  draft the visit note. You can ask to stop at any time.
- The note you see afterwards is **written and approved by your clinician**,
  not generated on its own.

## After the visit

- Your clinician reviews and edits the draft, then exports the final note —
  usually as a PDF for your records.
- KTB Health does **not** diagnose you, recommend treatments, or give medical
  advice. It records what your clinician documents.

## If you have questions

Your clinician and their clinic are responsible for how your data is handled.
For privacy specifics, see [Your data and privacy](/docs/patients/your-data-and-privacy)
or ask your care team directly.
