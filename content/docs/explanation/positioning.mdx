KTB Health is deliberately scoped: it is a **documentation** tool, not a
clinical decision tool.

## The rule

The physician decides; KTB records. Product copy must never diagnose, suggest
treatment, or make recommendations — it records what the clinician documents.
This is tracked as task T-021 in the org ledger and applies to UI strings,
marketing copy, and docs alike.

## Consequences

- No diagnostic or triage features, ever.
- AI-assisted features (e.g. transcription) produce drafts the clinician
  reviews and owns.
- Copy review treats "recommendation-shaped" language as a bug.
