Reference for how KTB turns a consultation into text — and what it does with
the audio afterwards.

## Languages

Transcription is tuned for **Portuguese (pt-BR)** consults. Speaker turns are
labeled automatically so the transcript reads as a conversation, not a wall of
text.

<Callout type="info">
  Working in another language? Transcription quality is best in Portuguese —
  test a short session before relying on it for a full consult.
</Callout>

## How transcription works

KTB uses a **client-first** strategy:

1. **Browser transcription** runs in the browser as you speak — fast and
   private-by-default.
2. **Confidence check.** After the session, KTB measures how much actual speech
   was captured versus transcript output.
3. **Server fallback.** If confidence is suspiciously low (very little speech
   detected, or almost no transcript text), the recorded audio is sent for
   server-side re-transcription with a larger speech model.

You never have to choose — the fallback is automatic. When it triggers, the
consultation keeps both versions: the raw server transcript and an
LLM-cleaned transcript, clearly labeled as machine output pending your review.

## Accuracy

- Expect a good first draft, not a perfect one — names, medications, and
  numbers are exactly what you should proofread.
- The silence/noise gate prevents the model from inventing phrases during
  quiet moments.
- Speaker labels can be wrong in overlapping speech; fix them in review.

## What happens to the audio

- Session audio is stored encrypted-at-rest in Cloudflare R2, keyed to the
  consultation, so the server pipeline can re-transcribe when needed.
- Audio is **never shown** in error messages — pipeline failures report a
  reason, not the recording.
- The cleaned transcript is explicitly marked as machine-corrected and **not**
  physician-approved until you say so.

## FAQ

**Does transcription work offline?** Browser transcription needs network for
model assets on first use; the server fallback requires connectivity. Plan for
online consults.

**Can I delete the audio?** Audio retention follows your clinic's data
practices — see [Privacy and consent](/en/docs/doctors/privacy-and-consent) and
ask your administrator about retention policy.

**Why does the transcript look different after processing?** You are seeing
the server-side re-transcription plus an LLM cleanup pass. Compare it with the
browser transcript on the detail page and keep whichever reading is accurate —
or edit directly.
