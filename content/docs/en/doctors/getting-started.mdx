This tutorial walks through recording your first consultation with KTB Health.
By the end you will have a structured, reviewable note you can export.

<Callout type="info" title="Documentation only">
  KTB Health records what you document. It does not diagnose, suggest
  treatment, or make recommendations — every draft is yours to review and own.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- A KTB Health account and a patient record (create one from the patients
  section if needed).
- A working microphone and a quiet room.

## Steps

1. **Open a new consultation.** From the consultations area, start a new
   consult and pick the patient it belongs to.

2. **Declare consent.** Before recording, confirm the patient has consented to
   the session being documented. KTB records the moment you declare consent —
   this is part of the LGPD audit trail. See
   [Privacy and consent](/en/docs/doctors/privacy-and-consent).

3. **Record.** Start the session. KTB transcribes the conversation as it
   happens — speaker turns are labeled (clinician, patient, others) so the
   transcript stays readable.

4. **Stop and review.** End the recording. The transcript and a structured
   anamnesis draft appear for review. Edit anything that does not match what
   was said — the machine output is a draft, not a final record.

5. **Approve and export.** Once the note reads correctly, finalize it and
   export to PDF for the patient record or referral.

## What to expect

- Transcription runs in the browser first; if confidence is low, the audio is
  re-transcribed server-side automatically. Details in
  [Transcription and audio](/en/docs/doctors/transcription-and-audio).
- A consultation stays editable until you finalize it — there is no silent
  overwrite of your approved note.

## Verify

- The consultation detail page shows the transcript, the structured anamnesis,
  and an export action.
- The PDF export opens and contains the note you approved.
