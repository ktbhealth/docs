KTB Health is built around a simple position: **the physician decides, KTB
records**. This page explains what that means for patient data.

## What is recorded

- **The consultation note** — transcript and structured anamnesis, edited and
  approved by you.
- **Session audio** — when capture is enabled, audio is stored securely so the
  transcription pipeline can do its job (see
  [Transcription and audio](/en/docs/doctors/transcription-and-audio)).
- **Consent timestamp** — the moment you declare patient consent is kept as an
  audit trail (LGPD).

## What KTB does not do

- It does not diagnose, triage, or recommend treatment — anywhere, ever.
- It does not make clinical decisions; every draft is machine output that a
  clinician reviews and owns.
- It is not a replacement for the medical record system your clinic is
  legally required to maintain — it's a documentation tool that feeds it.

## Consent

Recording an encounter requires the patient's consent. You declare it in the
app before capture starts, and that declaration is timestamped. If consent is
withdrawn or wasn't given, don't record — the feature is designed around that
gate, not around bypassing it.

## Your clinic's responsibilities

KTB provides the tooling; your practice remains the data controller. That means
your clinic's privacy policy, retention schedule, and patient notices apply.
Work with your administrator on retention and access policies — KTB enforces
access control and keeps the consent trail, but policy is yours to set.
