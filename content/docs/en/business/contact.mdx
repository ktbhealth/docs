## For clinics and partners

For pilots, procurement questions, security reviews, or deployment discussions,
open a conversation through the org's GitHub presence or your existing KTB
contact — mention which of these you need:

- **Pilot / evaluation** — scoped rollout for a clinic or team.
- **Procurement & compliance** — security questionnaire, DPA, retention
  requirements.
- **Deployment** — private cloud, self-hosted, or non-Cloudflare targets.
- **Integration** — FHIR/HL7 or EHR interop (work in progress — see the
  `interop-*` packages in the product repo).

## For developers

Contributions and technical questions go through the
[product repo](https://github.com/ktbhealth/product) — see the
[Contribute guide](/en/docs/developers/apps/tutorials/contribute).

## Responsible disclosure

Security reports: please do **not** open a public issue. Use a private channel
(GitHub Security Advisories on the product repo, or direct contact) and we'll
respond promptly.

<Callout type="info" title="Public contact details coming soon">
  A dedicated business inbox is being set up. Until then, GitHub is the
  canonical public channel for the org.
</Callout>
