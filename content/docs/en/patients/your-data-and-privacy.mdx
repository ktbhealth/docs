Honest, plain-language version of where your data goes.

## Who holds your data

Your **clinic is the data controller** — they decide why and how your
information is collected, under laws like Brazil's LGPD (and equivalent
regulations where you live). KTB Health provides the software the clinic uses.

## What may be collected

When a clinician uses KTB Health with your consent, the system may process:

- The audio of the consultation (stored securely, used for transcription).
- The transcript and the structured visit note.
- A record that consent was given, and when.

## What KTB does with it

- Transcription and note-structuring happen to produce a **draft** your
  clinician reviews.
- The software does not sell data, show ads, or use your information for its
  own purposes.
- The physician decides — KTB records. No diagnosis or treatment suggestions
  are generated for you.

## How it is protected

- **Consent first.** Capture only starts after you give explicit consent.
- **Under your clinician's control.** The consultation audio stays under the
  control of your clinician and clinic.
- **Can stay inside the clinic.** KTB Health can also be deployed in the
  clinic's own environment (self-hosting, by arrangement), so data does not
  have to leave the institution.
- **Synthetic data in demos and tests.** Real patient data is never used to
  demonstrate or test the product.
- **LGPD by architecture, not by disclaimer.** Privacy is built into how the
  system works, not added as a notice at the end.

## Your rights

Ask your clinic about accessing, correcting, or deleting your records — those
requests go to them, the controller. You can also decline recording entirely;
your clinician can document without it.
