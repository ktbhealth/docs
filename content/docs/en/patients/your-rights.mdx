Whatever country you're in, you have rights over your health information.
This page describes them in plain language — your clinic is the one who
must honor them, and KTB gives them the tooling to do it.

## In the United States

If your care team is a US covered entity, the **HIPAA Privacy Rule** gives
you the right to:

- **Access** — inspect and get a copy of your records, including the visit
  notes created with KTB.
- **Amend** — request corrections to records you believe are inaccurate or
  incomplete.
- **An accounting of disclosures** — know who your information was shared
  with.
- **Restrictions** — ask that certain uses or disclosures be limited.

KTB is not itself a HIPAA covered entity — your clinic is. The software
supports these rights (exportable records, audit trails, access controls),
but honoring them is the clinic's obligation.

## Everywhere else

- **Brazil (LGPD):** you can confirm, access, correct, anonymize, delete,
  and port your data. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
- **EU/UK (GDPR):** similar rights — access, rectification, erasure,
  portability, and the right to object.

Your care team should tell you which rules apply to you.

## How to exercise them

1. **Ask your clinic directly** — they are the data controller and handle
   these requests.
2. **Request your records** — clinicians using KTB can export your file in
   FHIR R4 or openEHR format; ask for it in whatever form is convenient.
3. **Decline recording** — you can always refuse transcription. Your
   clinician can document without it.

## If something goes wrong

If you believe your rights were violated, contact your care team first.
If unresolved, you can escalate to your local health-data authority (HHS
OCR in the US, ANPD in Brazil, your national DPA in the EU).
