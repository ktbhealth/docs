This tutorial adds audit coverage to a mutation: resolve the `Audit`
service optionally, record after success, and verify the entry in the
console.

## What you'll build

Every `updateSettings` call in your service writes an `admin_audit` row —
without audit ever being able to break the mutation.

## Steps

1. **Resolve the service optionally.** Inside your `Effect.gen` service:

   ```ts
   const audit = yield* Effect.serviceOption(Audit); // from @ktb/audit
   ```

   Optional on purpose — the plugin may not be registered.

2. **Record after the write succeeds.** Call
   `audit.value.record({ action: "settings.update", targetType: "settings",
   targetId: ..., detail: JSON.stringify({...}) })`. `record` never fails —
   no try/catch needed (see
   [record audit entries](/en/docs/developers/plugins/audit/how-to/record-audit-entries)).

3. **Check the console.** As a platform admin, open `/platform/audit` —
   newest-first, filterable by action/actor. Non-admins get a hard 404.

## Verify

```bash
bun run check
```

Confirm the row appears with the right actor (from `Principal`, not your
input), and that forcing an audit insert failure still lets the mutation
succeed.
